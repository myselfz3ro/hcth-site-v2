# HCTH — Master Pre-Arrival Plan v2 (post-simulation)
Revised from v1 after MiroFish 7-market swarm. Change driver: #1 objection = "good, or just cheap/gimmick?"
Fix = lead with AUTHENTICITY + 9.6★/883, price only as reassurance.

## MARKET TIERING (budget follows this)
- **TIER 1 — launch first (English + DE):** UK, Ireland, Wales, Netherlands, Germany.
  Simulation positive-leaning; lowest effort, highest return.
- **TIER 2 — launch after Tier 1 proves out (localised):** Belgium (NL+FR split), France (FR + chef story + TheFork).
  Need language + authenticity proof + a French review platform; higher effort.

## THE MARK (unchanged, this is working)
Momo steam + naan from tandoor, warm tones · 9.6★ · 883 reviews · one slogan.
- Tier-1 emotive slogan (UK/IE/DE): "Taste the Himalayas — in the heart of Tenerife."
- NL concrete variant: "The momo they fly back for. 9.6★, 883 reviews."
Mnemonic dish = MOMO everywhere.

## MESSAGE HIERARCHY (revised order)
1. AUTHENTIC Nepalese + Indian (momo, thakali — "real, not tourist tikka").
2. PROOF: 9.6★ from 883 diners.
3. REASSURE: €10–20, tourist tax included, vegan options, dog-friendly, choose your spice.
Price is never the headline.

## PER-MARKET EXECUTION
- **UK/IE/Wales (EN):** "best curry on the island" + momo + 883 reviews. Humour ok. Wales: one "Croeso" organic nod.
- **Netherlands (EN):** concrete, no fluff. Food + price + reviews. Direct CTA.
- **Germany (DE):** DE creative; SHOW explicit vegan dishes, exact hours, online reservation link; emphasise reliability/book-ahead.
- **Belgium (NL+FR):** two creatives; quality/authenticity over price.
- **France (FR):** chef/origin story, authenticity, FR language; add TheFork/LaFourchette listing; drop salesy tone.

## CAPABILITY GAPS TO CLOSE (surfaced by simulation)
1. **Online reservation** — DE/NL want to book ahead; pre-arrival intent dies without a bookable action. (Google Business "reserve" + a simple form/WhatsApp.)
2. **Explicit vegan dish list** — publish named vegan dishes (DE/Berlin, foodies).
3. **French review presence** — TheFork listing for FR/BE before Tier 2.
4. **Confirm tourist-tax-included** messaging (IE/UK value anxiety).
