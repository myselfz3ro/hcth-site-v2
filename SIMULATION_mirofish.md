# HCTH — MiroFish Swarm Simulation (7 markets)
Method: MiroFish-pattern reasoning simulation (model role-play, NOT real users — internal sanity check only).

shown to someone PLANNING a Tenerife trip 45–90 days out. Each market = 3 personas, first-5-sec reaction.
Facts pre-verified (Step 0): 9.6★/883, €10–20, momo/vegan/dog-friendly, Costa del Silencio — all confirmed.

- "Skeptical repeat-Tenerife visitor, Cardiff (Wales)" — "Every bar out there claims best curry." BUT 9.6★/883 is unusually high → "ok that's a lot of reviews, genuine." POSITIVE-conditional (the review count does the convincing).
- "Budget-conscious family, Birmingham" — "€10–20, dog-friendly, kids can pick mild spice? Sold, saving for week 2." POSITIVE.
VERDICT: 3/3 positive. Strongest market. Keep English, keep "best curry", lead with momo+review count. Welsh: no separate language needed, but a "Croeso / Welcome" nod in one organic post earns goodwill.
