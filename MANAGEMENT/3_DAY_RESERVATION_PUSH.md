# 3-Day Sprint: Client Reservation Push

## Objective
Generate reservation demand and convert to confirmed covers over the next 72 hours.
Budget: €0 (Organic/GBP Focus).

## Strategy (The "Reservation" Loop)
1. **Direct Call to Action (CTA):** Shift all content focus to one message: "Table booked = peace of mind in Costa del Silencio."
2. **Google Business Profile (GBP) Focus:** If GBP access is currently unreachable, drive all traffic to the live landing site's WhatsApp/Call link.
3. **The "Reservation" Offer:** 
   - Free appetizer (e.g., Poppadoms + Chutney) for all pre-booked tables.
   - Mention code "Tenerife-Ready" to validate effectiveness.

## Execution (3 Days)
- Day 1: "Booking ahead" launch post (FB/IG/GBP).
- Day 2: Dish focus (Momo/Tandoor) + Booking link.
- Day 3: Social proof (4.9★/276 reviews) + "Limited tables left."

## Targets
- Goal: Secure first 70 reservations.
- Monitor: Website link-clicks for WhatsApp/Call.
