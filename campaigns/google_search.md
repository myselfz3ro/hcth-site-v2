# HCTH — Google Campaigns (Search + Demand Gen + YouTube)
Goal: appear whenever someone researches Tenerife eating/holiday, then follow them until arrival.

## CAMPAIGN 1 — Search (planning + arrival intent)
Objective: capture active "where to eat" searchers. Budget start: €8–12/day.
Geo: UK, Ireland, Germany (planning) + Tenerife South 15km radius (arrival). Language: EN, DE, ES.

### Ad groups + keywords (phrase/exact)
- **Tenerife south dining:** "where to eat tenerife south", "best restaurants costa del silencio", "restaurants las galletas", "golf del sur restaurants"
- **Cuisine intent:** "indian restaurant tenerife south", "curry tenerife", "nepalese restaurant tenerife", "momo tenerife"
- **Arrival/near-me:** "restaurants near me" (geo Costa del Silencio), "best curry near me tenerife"
- **Trip planning:** "things to do costa del silencio", "where to eat in tenerife"
Negatives: jobs, recipe, delivery-only, cheap buffet, -franchise
