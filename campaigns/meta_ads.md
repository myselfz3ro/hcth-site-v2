# HCTH — Meta Campaigns (Facebook + Instagram)
Goal: plant the restaurant during trip-planning, build 3–7 exposures, convert to pre-arrival intent.

## AUDIENCE STACK
**A. Cold — travel intent (DREAMING/PLANNING):**
- Location: UK, Ireland, Germany, mainland Spain (NOT in Tenerife yet)
- Detailed targeting: Interested in "Tenerife", "Canary Islands", "Tenerife holidays", "Jet2holidays / TUI / Ryanair", + Travel behaviours "Frequently travels" / "Returned from trip"
- Layer: foodies — "Indian cuisine", "Vegan", "Foodies"
- Age 25–60. This is the plant-the-seed audience.

**B. Cold — lookalike:** 1–3% lookalike of past diners / page engagers / video viewers.

**C. Warm — RETARGETING (the recall engine):**
- Video viewers 25%+ (last 90d), IG/FB profile engagers, website visitors, "saved" posts
- This is where the "subconscious mark" is built via sequential ads.

**D. Hot — ARRIVAL:** people physically in Tenerife South (location = Costa del Silencio/Arona 10km) → "reserve tonight".

## SEQUENTIAL RETARGETING (tell a 3-act story, don't repeat one ad)
- **Act 1 (cold A/B) — DREAM:** reel of momo steam + island vibe. Caption "Taste the Himalayas — in the heart of Tenerife." Objective: video views / reach. Cheap, wide.
- **Act 2 (warm C, saw Act 1) — TRUST:** "9.6★ · 883 reviews · the momo they fly back for." Diner quotes. Objective: engagement/traffic.
- **Act 3 (warm C, saw Act 2) — ACT:** "Heading to Tenerife? Save our number, book your table. +34 641 11 45 11 / message us." Objective: messages / calls.
- **Act 4 (hot D) — NOW:** "You're here 🌴 Table tonight? Open till 11pm." Objective: messages/directions.

## CREATIVE RULES (so it sticks)
- Same signature look EVERY time: momo/tandoor, warm tones, logo, 9.6★ badge, the ONE slogan.
- Reels > static (cheaper reach, higher recall). Film: momo steam, naan-from-tandoor pull, curry pour, spice-level choice, happy table.
- First 1 sec must show food (thumb-stopper). Add captions (sound-off viewing).
- Frequency cap 2–3/week per person. Refresh creative every 2–3 wks to avoid fatigue.

## BUDGET (start lean, scale winners)
- Act 1 cold: €5–8/day · Act 2–3 retargeting: €4–6/day · Act 4 arrival: €2–3/day
- Total ~€12–18/day to start. Scale the ad sets with lowest cost-per-result after 7 days.
- ⚠️ Any ad spend = finance action → needs founder approval before activating paid budget.

## ORGANIC (free, 3–5x/week)
- IG Reels + FB: dish reels, behind-the-scenes, review screenshots, "spice level 1–5" series.
- Hashtags (from ad_and_social_copy.md): #CostaDelSilencio #TenerifeFood #TenerifeSouth #NepaleseFood #ThingsToDoTenerife #VisitTenerife
- Instagram "Guides" / Highlights: "Planning Tenerife? Save this." = travelers save during planning = free re-exposure.
- Pinterest: pin food photos to "Tenerife food / things to do Tenerife" — travelers save these months ahead.
