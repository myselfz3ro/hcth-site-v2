# HCTH - 1 EUR/day Micro-Test Campaign (LAUNCH-READY)
Purpose: tiny daily budget, watch if clients click/engage, THEN decide to scale. 3-4 day test.
NOTE: turning this on moves money -> needs founder GO + amount each time. This file is the ready setup, NOT active.

## WHY META ONLY AT 1 EUR/DAY (honest)
- Meta (FB/IG) minimum ~1 EUR/day for reach/engagement/traffic objectives -> works.
- Google Search clicks cost 0.40-0.90 EUR each -> 1 EUR/day buys ~1 click, auction skips you -> DON'T run Google at 1 EUR. Save Google for when budget >= 10 EUR/day.
- So: 1 channel, Meta, 1 EUR/day, 1 ad set, 1 clear creative. Clean signal.

## EXACT SETUP (copy into Meta Ads Manager / Boost)
Option A - simplest (Instagram/FB Boost Post), 2 taps:
1. Post the Day-1 reel (momo steam) organically.
2. Boost it -> Budget 1 EUR/day -> Duration 4 days (total 4 EUR) -> Goal "More Messages" or "More Website visits" (link: https://myselfz3ro.github.io/hcth-tenerife/).
3. Audience "Advantage/Automatic" OR the custom one below.

Option B - proper campaign (Ads Manager):
- Campaign objective: Traffic (to website) or Engagement (cheap reach/recall).
- Budget: 1.00 EUR/day, CBO on. Run 4 days.
- Ad set 1 - "Tenerife planners":
  - Locations: United Kingdom, Ireland (start with 2 only - keeps 1 EUR focused)
  - Age 28-60, all genders
  - Detailed targeting: Interests = Tenerife OR Canary Islands OR Jet2holidays OR TUI; narrow with Indian cuisine OR Foodie
  - Placements: Advantage+ (Reels + Feed + Stories)
- Ad: the momo reel + this primary text:
  "Taste the Himalayas - in the heart of Tenerife. 9.6 stars from 883 diners. Authentic Nepalese & Indian in Costa del Silencio. Save us for your trip."
  Headline: "Costa del Silencio's 9.6-star curry house"
  CTA button: "Send Message" (WhatsApp) or "Learn More" (website)
  Link: https://myselfz3ro.github.io/hcth-tenerife/

## WHAT 1 EUR/DAY REALISTICALLY BUYS (4 days, ~4 EUR total)
- Reach ~ 400-1,200 people total
- Link clicks ~ 5-25, OR messages ~ 2-8
- NOT enough for bookings proof - but ENOUGH to read the signal:
  -> Is cost-per-click cheap (<0.30 EUR = great) or expensive (>0.80 EUR = wrong audience/creative)?
  -> Which of UK vs IE engages more?
  -> Does the momo reel stop the thumb (view-rate >25% = strong)?

## DECISION RULE AFTER 4 DAYS (what we learn)
- CPC < 0.30 EUR AND messages coming in -> WINNER, scale to 5-10 EUR/day, add Google Search.
- CPC 0.30-0.80 EUR, some engagement -> tweak creative/audience, retest 1-2 EUR/day.
- CPC > 0.80 EUR, nothing -> change creative first (not budget). The ad, not the money, is the problem.

## MEASUREMENT
Track: daily cost, reach, CPC, link clicks, messages/WhatsApp, "saw us online?" asked at tables.
Once 3-4 days of numbers exist -> TimesFM forecasts the flow + tells us scale-or-kill.
