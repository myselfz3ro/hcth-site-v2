# HCTH — "Decide Before They Fly" Strategy
Plant the restaurant in the tourist's mind during trip-planning, stay there until they land, convert on arrival.
Assets: Google Search (owned), Facebook, Instagram. Market: UK 37% · Ireland · Germany 11% · mainland Spain — 45–90 day planning window.

## THE PRINCIPLE (why this works, not gimmicks)
1. **Mere-exposure effect** — people prefer what they've seen before. Repeated, consistent exposure during planning = "I already know that place" on arrival. This is the "subconscious mark."
2. **Decision-made-early** — a Tenerife trip is dreamed/planned 1.5–3 months out. If we're the curry place they saved/remembered BEFORE booking flights, we beat every in-destination competitor who only advertises once tourists land.
3. **One sticky asset, repeated** — same hook, same signature image, same slogan across every channel. Repetition of ONE idea sticks; variety dilutes. (See brand assets below.)

## THE TRAVELER FUNNEL — where we hit them
| Stage | When | Their mindset | Our move |
|---|---|---|---|
| DREAMING | 60–90d before | "Should we do Tenerife?" | IG/FB reels + Pinterest: aspirational food/island imagery. Plant the name. |
| PLANNING | 30–60d | "What's there to do/eat?" | Google Search ads on "where to eat Tenerife south", "best restaurant Costa del Silencio"; blog SEO. Retarget dreamers. |
| BOOKING | 14–30d | "Locking the trip in" | Retargeting ad: social proof (9.6★/883) + "save our number / reserve now". |
| PRE-TRIP | 1–14d | "Making a list" | Retargeting: "See you this week — book your table." Google Business "reserve". |
| ARRIVAL | in-destination | "Hungry, where?" | Google Maps local pack #1 + "near me" + geofenced reminder to warm audiences. |

Key insight: **90% of competitors only fight at ARRIVAL.** We win by owning DREAMING→PLANNING so the choice is already made.

## BRAND ASSETS (the mark — use identically EVERYWHERE)
- **Signature image:** steam rising off fresh momo + naan being pulled from the tandoor (fire). Warm/orange tones. Always the same style.
- **Slogan options (pick ONE, use forever):**
  - "Taste the Himalayas — in the heart of Tenerife." ← recommended (ties craving + place)
  - "The curry worth flying for."
  - "Your Tenerife isn't complete without it."
- **Mnemonic dish:** the MOMO. Own it. "The momo place in Costa del Silencio." One dish = one memory hook.
- **Consistent logo, colours, the 9.6★/883 badge** on every creative.

## THE OMNIPRESENCE PLAY — "whenever they search Tenerife, we're there"
1. **Google Search** — bid on Tenerife-eating intent keywords (see campaigns/google_search.md).
2. **Google Display + Demand Gen + YouTube** — remarket to anyone who searched Tenerife food / visited our site, so we follow them across the web & YouTube with the signature creative.
3. **Meta (FB+IG)** — target travel-intent audiences planning Tenerife/Canary Islands trips + lookalikes of our diners + retarget video-viewers (see campaigns/meta_ads.md).
4. **Pinterest** (free + paid) — "Tenerife food", "things to do Tenerife" boards; travelers SAVE pins during planning = free re-exposure.
5. **Organic SEO/content** — rank for "best restaurant Costa del Silencio / Tenerife south curry" so we appear even without ad spend.
6. **Google Business** — Maps local pack + "reserve a table" for the arrival moment.

## FREQUENCY = the actual mechanism
- Aim **3–7 exposures per person across the 90-day window** (enough to stick, not enough to annoy).
- Sequential retargeting: Ad 1 (dream/food) → Ad 2 (9.6★ social proof) → Ad 3 (book before you fly). Never show the same single ad 10x — tell a 3-step story.
- Frequency cap: ~2–3 impressions/week/person.

## MEASURE
- Pre-arrival bookings/calls (tag source), "saved" IG/Pinterest actions, Google Business "reserve"/direction clicks, ad frequency & reach, Maps ranking for target keywords, "mention you saw us online" at the table (ask + log).
